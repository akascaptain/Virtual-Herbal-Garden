Alovera

In Ayurveda, Aloe Vera (Kumari) is revered for its cooling, rejuvenating, and healing properties. It aids digestion, promotes skin health, boosts immunity, and balances Pitta and Vata doshas.

Ashwagandha

In Ayurveda, Ashwagandha is a powerful adaptogen known for reducing stress, improving strength, boosting energy, enhancing sleep, and rejuvenating the body. It balances Vata and Kapha doshas effectively.

Bel

In Ayurveda, the Bel plant (Bilva) is valued for its digestive, anti-inflammatory, and detoxifying properties. It treats diarrhea, dysentery, and boosts metabolism. Bel balances Kapha and Vata doshas.

Money

In Ayurveda, the Money Plant (Epipremnum aureum) is known for purifying air and reducing stress. Though not medicinally prominent, it's believed to attract positivity, enhance mood, and promote environmental harmony.

Neem

In Ayurveda, Neem is a potent herb with antibacterial, antiviral, and antifungal properties. It detoxifies the body, improves skin health, boosts immunity, and balances Pitta and Kapha doshas.

Sandlewood

In Ayurveda, Sandalwood (Chandan) is revered for its cooling, calming properties. It promotes skin health, relieves stress, enhances meditation, and balances Pitta dosha. Sandalwood also acts as an anti-inflammatory.

Tulsi

In Ayurveda, Tulsi (Holy Basil) is considered a sacred herb with immune-boosting, anti-inflammatory, and detoxifying properties. It balances all three doshas, supports respiratory health, and promotes mental clarity and longevity.

Monstera

Monstera is classical Ayurvedic herb, is valued in holistic wellness for purifying air, enhancing indoor aesthetics, and promoting calmness. It supports mental clarity and positive environmental energy.

Aglaonema

Aglaonema is known for purifying air, reducing stress, and enhancing indoor harmony. Its presence promotes calmness, well-being, and supports a positive living environment.

Underwater Plants

Underwater plants in Ayurveda, like Brahmi and Punarnava, are valued for enhancing memory, reducing inflammation, purifying blood, supporting kidney function, calming the mind, and promoting overall balance and vitality.

Banana Plants

In Ayurveda, banana plants aid digestion, soothe ulcers, and balance doshas. The fruit nourishes tissues, the flower helps in diabetes, and the stem supports kidney detox and weight loss.

House Plam Plants

In Ayurveda, palm plants like areca and date palms are used to enhance digestion, increase vitality, cool the body, support cardiovascular health, improve fertility, and balance Pitta dosha naturally.
