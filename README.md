🌿 Virtual Herbal Garden - A Digital Gateway to Medicinal Plant Knowledge
Welcome to the Virtual Herbal Garden!
This project bridges the gap between traditional healing practices and modern accessibility by creating a comprehensive, interactive digital platform for exploring medicinal plants used in the AYUSH sector.

Key Features:

🪴 Interactive 3D Models: Rotate, zoom, and explore realistic models of medicinal plants.

📚 Detailed Information: Learn about each plant's botanical name, common names, habitat, medicinal uses, and cultivation methods.

🎥 Multimedia Integration: High-quality images, educational videos, and audio descriptions for an enhanced experience.

🔎 Search and Filter Options: Easily find plants based on medicinal uses, region, or plant type.

🚶‍♂️ Virtual Tours: Thematic guided tours (e.g., plants for immunity, skin care, digestive health).

✨ User Interaction: Bookmark favourite plants, take notes, and share plant information on social media.

Objective:
To create an engaging, informative, and user-friendly Virtual Herbal Garden that promotes awareness and understanding of medicinal plants, making traditional knowledge accessible to students, practitioners, and enthusiasts from the comfort of their homes.

Expected Outcome:
An immersive, visually appealing educational tool that supports the spread of AYUSH sector knowledge through technology.
