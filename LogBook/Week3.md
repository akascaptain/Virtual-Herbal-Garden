# Virtual Herbal Garden – Week III  
### Literature Review & Research Phase  
**Duration:** Week III  

---

## Objective of the Week
The third week was dedicated to conducting an **extensive literature review and research phase** to establish a strong foundation for the Virtual Herbal Garden project. This stage focused on analyzing **existing virtual garden platforms, 3D model integration techniques, AR/VR applications, and multilingual educational tools**, particularly aligned with **AYUSH medicinal plant digitization** and **SIH 2024 project guidelines**.

---

## Detailed Activities Performed

### 1. **Comprehensive Literature Review**
- Conducted a systematic review of research papers, whitepapers, and projects related to:
  - **Virtual Herbal Gardens and Botanical Education Platforms**  
  - **Integration of AR/VR in Botanical Learning**  
  - **Use of GLB and GLTF formats for 3D visualization**  
  - **Multilingual platforms for AYUSH and traditional medicine education**  

- Sources reviewed included:
  - IEEE Xplore, Springer, and ResearchGate publications.
  - Government resources on AYUSH medicinal plants.
  - Existing virtual herbarium websites (e.g., Botanical Survey of India digital platforms).
  
- Key takeaway: There is **no single comprehensive platform** that integrates **multilingual support, AR/VR, 3D models, and real-time educational interaction** for AYUSH herbs.

---

### 2. **Comparative Study of Existing Solutions**
- Analyzed the shortcomings of existing platforms:
  - Most are **static web pages** without interactive 3D or AR capabilities.
  - Limited or no **multilingual integration** (English only in most cases).
  - Lack of **immersive virtual tours** for remote education.
  - Absence of **AI-powered recommendation and search features**.

- Benchmarked the proposed system against these gaps to ensure:
  - Integration of **GLB models for interactive 3D visualization**.
  - Support for **multilingual content (including regional Indian languages)**.
  - Compatibility with **React (frontend)** and **Flask/Django (backend)**.
  - Future readiness for **AI/ML-based plant recognition**.

---

### 3. **Research on Technology Stack**
- **Frontend Exploration:**  
  - React.js selected for **dynamic UI and seamless component integration**.
  - Tailwind CSS & ShadCN considered for a **clean, professional design**.

- **Backend Research:**  
  - Flask with SQLAlchemy identified for **lightweight and scalable backend**.
  - MySQL/XAMPP to manage herbal plant databases.

- **3D Model Integration:**  
  - Researched Three.js and Babylon.js for rendering **GLB/GLTF models**.
  - Tested sample GLB files for compatibility with React.

- **Virtual Tour Possibility:**  
  - Investigated WebXR and A-Frame frameworks for browser-based AR/VR.

---

### 4. **Research Methodology Applied**
- **PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews)** was partially followed for filtering relevant studies.
- Classified literature under:
  1. **Existing Herbal Garden Projects**  
  2. **AR/VR in Education**  
  3. **3D Web Visualization Techniques**  
  4. **Multilingual and Cultural Adaptation Strategies**

- Maintained a **literature matrix** summarizing each paper’s contribution, technology stack, and limitations.

---

## Decisions Taken
- **Finalized the core technologies** for implementation:  
  - **React + Flask + MySQL** as the base stack.
  - **GLB 3D models** for plant visualization.
  - **Multilingual support** using translation libraries (Google Translate API as a fallback).

- Established a **clear gap** in existing literature which our project aims to fill:
  - Lack of **AR/VR-based virtual herbal education platforms in India**.
  - Minimal **interactive learning methods for AYUSH herbs**.

---

## Challenges Faced
- Limited availability of **high-quality open-source 3D plant models**.  
- Conflicting research on the **best framework for AR/VR integration** (A-Frame vs Three.js).  
- Inconsistencies in plant data from multiple government databases.

---

## Outcome of the Week
- A detailed **literature review report (15+ references)** was drafted.
- Identified a **unique innovation angle**: immersive, multilingual, AI-ready platform.
- Prepared the groundwork for **Week IV: System Design & Architecture Blueprint**.
