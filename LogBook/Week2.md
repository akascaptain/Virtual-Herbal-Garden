# Week II – Problem Statement & Justification

### Duration: 04/03/2025 – 09/03/2025

### Objective of the Week
The objective for this week was to define the **problem statement** in clear technical terms and justify why the Virtual Herbal Garden project is necessary, feasible, and impactful. This stage aimed to provide a strong foundation for stakeholders, mentors, and SIH evaluators to understand the **real-world need** for the solution.

---

## Detailed Activities Performed

### 1. Problem Identification
- Despite India’s rich heritage of medicinal plants, the awareness and accessibility of this knowledge remain **fragmented and region-specific**.
- Major gaps identified:
  - Traditional medicinal knowledge is often **documented in localized formats** or restricted to expert practitioners.
  - Lack of **interactive, modern digital platforms** showcasing medicinal plants with multimedia content.
  - Absence of **search-based and user-friendly systems** for students, farmers, and common users to explore medicinal uses.
  - Minimal **integration of AR/VR technologies** in herbal education tools.

### 2. Stakeholder Analysis
- **Primary users:** Students, researchers, herbal practitioners, farmers, and general public.
- **Secondary users:** Government agencies (AYUSH, NMPB), healthcare institutions, and educational platforms.
- **Stakeholder needs:**
  - **Accurate data** on medicinal plants.
  - Easy **search-by-ailment and plant name** access.
  - **Multilingual interface** for rural and semi-urban areas.
  - **Interactive learning experience** using modern tools like AR/VR.

### 3. Data-backed Justification
- Consulted official databases such as:
  - **National Medicinal Plants Board (NMPB)** – Repository of 150+ medicinal plants.
  - **WHO Global Centre for Traditional Medicine** – Emphasizing the digital conservation of traditional medicine.
- Observed that most databases are:
  - Text-heavy and lack **visual or interactive elements**.
  - Do not provide **AR-based exploration** of plants.
  - Have **limited search and categorization features**.

### 4. Relevance to SIH 2024 & Ministry of AYUSH
- Problem Statement ID: 1555 focuses on creating a **Virtual Herbal Garden** to preserve and promote India's herbal wealth.
- Aligns with:
  - **Digital India Mission** – Enhancing citizen access to knowledge.
  - **AYUSH Ministry Goals** – Bridging traditional and modern medicine through technology.
  - **Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being)** – Encouraging natural healing and medicinal plant usage.

### 5. Proposed Solution Justification
- **Virtual Herbal Garden platform** addresses the identified challenges by:
  - Providing a **centralized, visually rich plant repository**.
  - Enabling **search-by-ailment**, **keyword mapping**, and **plant categorization**.
  - Using **AR.js & WebXR** for plant visualization in real-world environments.
  - Offering **multi-language support** (Marathi, Hindi, English initially, expandable).
  - Incorporating **quizzes and knowledge cards** for an interactive learning experience.
  - Future-ready for **AI chatbot** and **e-commerce integration**.

---

## Decisions Taken
1. Finalized the **problem definition**: “Limited awareness, accessibility, and interactive digital resources for medicinal plants in India.”
2. Decided to focus on **education, preservation, and interactive visualization** rather than only static plant listings.
3. Confirmed the **key modules** to address this problem:
   - Interactive Plant Database
   - Search & Filter by ailment and usage
   - AR/VR Integration
   - Quiz and Learning Module
   - Multilingual support system

---

## Challenges Faced
- Ensuring the **problem statement matched both SIH requirements and real-world gaps** was a challenge.
- Balancing **technical ambition (AR, AI chatbot, 3D models)** with **project timeline constraints**.
- Data verification: Many publicly available plant datasets lacked consistency or scientific references.

---

## Outcome of the Week
- A **clear and well-defined problem statement** aligned with the SIH 2024 challenge.
- Comprehensive **justification for building the Virtual Herbal Garden**, backed by data and stakeholder analysis.
- Documented a **feature prioritization roadmap**, ensuring essential features (database, search, AR) are delivered first, while advanced features (AI chatbot, offline mode) are planned for future iterations.
- Created a **concept note** to present to the project guide and mentor for validation.

---

## Technical Relevance
The problem statement and justification phase helped:
- Avoid scope creep by focusing on **essential, impactful features**.
- Build a foundation for technical modules like **Flask APIs, MySQL integration, ReactJS front-end, and AR/VR visualization**.
- Strengthen the **SIH presentation and evaluation** by addressing actual gaps in existing herbal plant systems.
