
# Week I – Title Selection

### Duration: 16/02/2025 – 02/03/2025

### Objective of the Week
The objective of this week was to finalize the project title and align it with the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2024** problem statement and the goals of the **Ministry of AYUSH**. This phase focused on selecting a title that is technically sound, innovative, and directly contributes to the conservation and digital transformation of India's traditional medicinal plant knowledge.

---

## Detailed Activities Performed

### 1. Problem Statement Exploration
- The team analyzed **Problem Statement ID: 1555 – Create a Virtual Herbal Garden**, provided on the SIH 2024 portal.
- Key aspects highlighted in the problem:
  - Need for a **virtual, interactive herbal plant database**.
  - Requirement to **showcase medicinal plants with their uses** in a modern, engaging way.
  - Focus on **multilingual support** for broader accessibility.
  - Potential integration of **Augmented Reality (AR) and 3D visualization**.

### 2. Research on Project Scope
- Researched existing herbal databases (e.g., NMPB, WHO, Ministry of AYUSH).
- Identified limitations in current systems:
  - Lack of **interactivity**.
  - Minimal **integration with modern web technologies** (e.g., AR/VR, mobile accessibility).
  - Absence of **multilingual content** catering to rural and semi-urban users.

### 3. Brainstorming Project Vision and Goals
- Conducted group discussions to align the project with:
  - **Educational purposes** (students, researchers, and the general public).
  - **Cultural preservation** (documenting traditional medicinal knowledge).
  - **Digital innovation** (AR/VR, search-by-ailment, 3D plant models, multilingual support).
  
- Proposed project goals:
  1. Develop a **Virtual Herbal Garden platform** with interactive 3D plant models.
  2. Enable **symptom-based search** to find relevant medicinal plants.
  3. Support **multiple Indian languages** (Marathi, Hindi, English initially).
  4. Include **educational tools** such as quizzes, plant cards, and a knowledge explorer.

### 4. Technology and Feasibility Consideration
- Evaluated technology stack options based on previous discussions:
  - **Backend:** Python (Flask) for lightweight and flexible API development.
  - **Frontend (Web):** ReactJS for a modular, responsive interface.
  - **AR/VR:** AR.js and WebXR for browser-based AR without heavy app installations.
  - **Mobile:** React Native for a future-ready hybrid mobile app.
  - **Database:** MySQL (integrated with Flask and XAMPP for development).
- Chose an **open-source first approach** to minimize costs and encourage community collaboration.

### 5. Title Finalization
After evaluating the problem scope, goals, and technology feasibility, the title **“Virtual Herbal Garden”** was finalized because:
- It reflects both the **virtualization** and **herbal medicinal plant** theme.
- It is **concise, descriptive, and professional**, aligning with AYUSH’s mission.
- It is **SIH-compliant** and resonates with the focus on **education, preservation, and innovation**.

---

## Decisions Taken
1. Selected the project title: **Virtual Herbal Garden**.
2. Confirmed alignment with SIH 2024 Problem Statement ID: 1555.
3. Finalized the core vision to integrate **AI, AR, and multilingual accessibility**.
4. Decided to adopt a **modular development approach**: Backend APIs → Plant Database → Frontend → AR Integration.

---

## Challenges Faced
- The initial challenge was defining the **exact scope of the project** since the problem statement allowed various interpretations (e.g., simple plant listing vs. a fully interactive platform).
- Selecting a **technology stack** that supports both **web and mobile** while allowing easy AR/VR integration required additional research.

---

## Outcome of the Week
- A clear, SIH-aligned **project title and vision statement**.
- Defined project objectives that include **education, digital conservation, and innovation**.
- Established a **technology roadmap** that would guide the coming weeks:
  - Week II: Problem Statement & Justification
  - Week III: Requirement Analysis
  - Week IV onwards: Data, backend, and AR integration.

- Set the foundation for **future features** like:
  - AI chatbot for health-based queries.
  - E-commerce integration for herbal product procurement.
  - Offline mobile version for rural outreach.
